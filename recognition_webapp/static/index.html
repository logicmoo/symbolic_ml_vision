Two-frame (prev \u2192 current)

Each current object matched to its previous-frame self by colour, area, and proximity; reports movement, rotation, occlusion. Stable e# ids. Inferences, not certainties.

Tracked layers (clip memory)

Persistent e# identities accumulated across the frames you have viewed; a reappearing object re-binds to its remembered (occluded) entity instead of being called new.

Frame diff (pixel-level) \u2014 low-level, click to expand

A pixel-level diff against the previous frame, before entity binding. Grey = stable, green = appeared, red = disappeared, yellow = changed in place. Regions carry competing hypotheses.
